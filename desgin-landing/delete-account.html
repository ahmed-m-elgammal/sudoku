Delete your account and data

You can delete everything ASSIZE holds about you in two steps. The first clears your device immediately; the second removes the server-side record by request, within 30 days.

Step 1 — Wipe the device (instant, self-serve)

In the app: Settings → Delete my data.
This wipes your local save, echoes, identity, preferences and session on this device, and mints a fresh guest identity so you can keep playing. No email or account is needed.

What is removed locally: campaign position, cosmetics, echoes, Ink and Season progress, Story flags, device-stored identity. These never reached the server unless you played ranked or daily.

Step 2 — Remove the server-side record (by request)

Server-side Standing, duel history, daily results and streaks, Ink audit ledger and recovery code link are keyed to your guest id, which never left your device. Because we hold no email, we cannot find your record without your help. To delete it:

Email privacy@assize.example
Include the guest id or your four-word recovery code (Settings → Recovery), with the subject line "Account deletion request". We delete the matching record within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you lost both the guest id and recovery code, we cannot locate your record and no data remains linked to you after your device wipe (Step 1) — the server record becomes orphaned and unidentifiable.

What is deleted vs. kept

  • Deleted (device): save game, cosmetics, echoes, preferences, identity (immediate).
  • Deleted (server, on request): Standing, duel history, daily results/streaks, Ink audit ledger, recovery code link, any anti-cheat rows tied to the guest id.
  • Kept: anonymised aggregate metrics (duel counts, bot timings) with no identifier, and back-up copies up to 35 days during the deletion window.
  • Retention period: device data immediately on Step 1; server data within 30 days of the request (max 35 days incl. back-ups).

Telemetry opt-out (no account deletion needed)

Settings → Minimal telemetry toggle. Anti-cheat security rows generated by the server (impossible placement intervals, uniform timing) cannot be switched off per account and are removed only with Step 2.

Children's data

ASSIZE is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child submitted data, contact privacy@assize.example and we will delete it.

Contact

Privacy: privacy@assize.example. Reference your guest id or recovery code so we can find your record.
