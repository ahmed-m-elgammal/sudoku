The duel, in thirty seconds

Same puzzle. Separate boards. One winner.

Placement, claims, wounds. Every rule of the Assize fits on the back of a chit; none of them forgive.

  • I
    Placement

    Tap a cell, tap a numeral. A wrong digit costs one Seal and Flinches your cooldowns for three seconds.

  • II
    Claims

    Complete a row, column or box to claim it: one Seal of damage, two if the claim is Clean. Wrong ink in that unit costs the bonus forever.

  • III
    Momentum

    Every correct placement shaves half a second off all your cooldowns. Speed is a weapon.

  • IV
    Rites

    Twelve abilities across four Orders, each with its own cooldown; the first cast of each begins at half cooldown. Statuses run on a four-second global gap and immunity rules.

  • V
    A judgment

    Seal death, then Reckoning (fill the Tablet), then Sudden Judgment at ten minutes, then forfeit. In that order. Always.

The four Orders

Choose your ink. Each wears a different wound.

Free via play. Apothecary unlocks after Folio II, Warden after Folio IV. All four are viable; none is the answer.

Scholar

Marginalia : first mistake forgiven.

  • Augur : reveal a digit
  • Unseal : clear statuses, 6 s immunity
  • Fair Copy : auto-candidates for a box

Executioner

Last Rites : claims deal +1 when foe is at three Seals or fewer.

  • Sever : Chain their tightest unit
  • Hush : their number pad dies for 2.5 s
  • Reckoning : next claim deals +1

Apothecary

Distiller : statuses you cast last two seconds longer.

  • Smudge : five digits blur for 7 s
  • Tincture : +1 Seal, twice per duel
  • Miasma : erase their notes for 10 s

Warden

Bulwark : the first incoming status is negated.

  • Ward : negate the next status for 15 s
  • Mirror : reflect the next status for 10 s
  • Quarantine : deny a unit for 12 s

Ways to play

One engine. Six doors.

Every mode runs on the same seeded, deterministic engine. Practice and campaign duels need no server; ranked, daily and friends do.

ranked-by-rung

Endless Assize

Wins ascend; losses send you back to the foot. Best rung is forever. A Magistrate waits every third rung.

personal

Your Shade

Your newest Echo is mined: pace, burned ink, rite density, turned back against you at your own tempo.

daily

Daily Assize

One seeded Tablet per UTC day, same for every soul. Plus ten seconds per mistake. Top one hundred globally.

campaign

The Nine Folios

Twenty-seven duels with arcs, interludes, and the adaptive ninth. The story bible closes here.

connection

Ranked and friends

Server matchmaking with widening Standing windows and a four-second Shade fallback. Friend duels use a code; sharing an Echo mints a sealed chit with no name on it.

solo

Practice

Any difficulty, hints allowed, small daily Ink cap. The honest room where you learn the rites before you trust them.
