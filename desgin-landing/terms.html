The Assize is a court of manners as well as of logic. Play fair, do your own ink, and the Ledger will remember you kindly.

2. Accounts and identity

Guest play requires no email or password. A recovery code restores server-known progress on another device. Losing the code is not recoverable. If payments ever ship, they will be tied to the same guest identity and subject to these terms.

3. Fair play

Ranked and daily modes exist to measure clerks against each other. Using solver software, injection tools, modified clients or automation to submit moves is prohibited and detectable to a degree. We apply speed, uniformity and plausibility sanity checks and shadow-queue suspicious results. Repeated or flagrant abuse results in exclusion from leaderboards and ranked queues. The honest framing, from the design itself: a solver feeding moves at human pace is undetectable. We would rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise.

4. Multiplayer conduct

Friend and ranked duels are unruly only in ink. Do not harass opponents, spam friend codes, share exploits, or attempt to desynchronise a duel. Reports are welcome.

5. Leaderboards, streaks and Ink

Standing, streaks and daily results live on our servers and are authoritative. Ink awards are verified against server duel logs — replayed, foreign or unverifiable claims are dropped without credit — and Standing is computed server-side from logged duels. Leaderboard entries may be pruned or corrected when evidence of cheating or a bug exists.

6. Echoes and sealed chits

Recorded duels and chits carry gameplay only. The privacy pass redacts names at both export and import, so a chit cannot be used to identify or contact another clerk. Do not attempt to circumvent the redaction.

7. User-generated and shared content

Echo chits, friend codes and recovery codes are short strings. Do not embed abusive, unlawful or harassing text where free-form fields or chit wrappers allow it. You remain responsible for what you send.
