You were Magister Orsolo's clerk when they hanged him for forging the Great Ledger. Now an ink-echo wearing his voice — a Shade — teaches you the Reckoning.

ASSIZE is a gothic 1v1 sudoku duel where law is settled by solving. Both pleaders face the same sealed Tablet. Wrong ink cracks your own wax Seals; completing a row, a column or a box claims it and wounds your foe. The first pleader to break the other's Seals — or fill the Tablet — prevails.

THE DUEL
· Race one sealed 9×9 sudoku Tablet against a living opponent or the Shade
· Claim rows, columns and boxes — each claim wounds the pleader across the aisle
· Wrong ink is punished: every error cracks your own wax Seals
· First to shatter the foe's Seals — or to fill the Tablet — wins the plea

RITES AND RELICS
· Cast engraved rites to hinder your rival: Chains, Smudges, Hushes, Miasmas, Quarantines
· Bear passive relics that answer the rites of others
· Shade Echoes — duel the recorded ink of any past duel, including your own
