     4→PITCH
     5→A 1v1 sudoku duel in a plague-walled medieval city where law is settled by solving. Both players race the same sealed puzzle, claim rows, columns and boxes to wound each other, and cast engraved "rites" to hinder one another. Adult, grim, restrained, like a woodcut grimoire.     48→2. CORE MECHANICS (implement exactly; expose all numbers in /shared/config.ts)
    49→======================================================================
    50→PUZZLE: Standard 9x9 with a unique solution. Both duelists get the same seeded puzzle. Clients receive givens only; the solution lives on the server (and locally in solo and Shade modes). Include a generator and a technique-based grader.
    51→ Difficulty tiers: Easy 36-40 givens (singles only), Medium 30-35, Hard 26-29 (pairs and pointing), Expert 22-25 (X-wing and chains).
    52→
    53→SEALS (HP): Each duelist has 7 wax Seals (8 for Magistrates).
    54→
    55→PLACING A DIGIT: Select a cell, tap a numeral. Validate against the solution.
    56→ - Correct: digit is final (no undo). Tiny ink-settle animation.
    57→ - Wrong: lose 1 Seal, red brush strike across the cell, shake, the digit clears after 1.0s. Cooldowns pause for 3s ("Flinch").
    58→ - Pencil mode lets you note candidates (never costs Seals). Erase works only on notes and wrong digits.
    59→
    60→CLAIMS: The first duelist to complete a unit (row, column or box) claims it and deals damage to the opponent.
    61→ - Damage: 1 Seal. 2 Seals if "Clean" (the claimer made zero mistakes in that unit this duel and used no Augur reveal in it).
    62→ - A unit can be claimed only once per duel. Completing an already-claimed unit earns Ink only.
    63→ - Show the claim as a wax stamp in the board margin: rows in the right gutter, columns in the bottom gutter, boxes at the box corner. Your stamp is oxblood wax with a fleur-de-lis. The opponent's is black wax with a tau cross. Different shapes, so it never depends on color alone.
    64→
    65→MOMENTUM: Each correct placement reduces all your ability cooldowns by 0.5s.
    66→
    67→WIN CONDITIONS (checked in this order):
    68→ 1. Opponent reaches 0 Seals.
    69→ 2. First to correctly complete the whole grid wins "by Reckoning".
    70→ 3. Sudden Judgment at 10:00: more Seals wins; tie, more claimed units; tie, fewer mistakes; tie, Draw.
    71→ 4. Opponent forfeits or is disconnected beyond the 20s grace.
    72→
    73→STATUS EFFECTS (apply to the target's board and only to cells that make sense):
    74→ - CHAIN: an empty cell becomes uneditable for its duration. A brass padlock and chain overlay is shown. Cannot target givens or filled cells.
    75→ - SMUDGE: N of the target's own placed (non-given) digits blur and cannot be read. They still count as placed.
    76→ - HUSH: the number pad is unresponsive for its duration (cells can still be selected). A wax finger-seal overlays the pad.
    77→ - MIASMA: pencil notes are erased and pencil mode is disabled for its duration.
    78→ - QUARANTINE: a chosen unit on the target's board cannot be claimed by them for 12s. Shown as a hazard-marked gutter.
    79→ Anti-frustration: one active status per type; 4s global gap between incoming statuses; 5s immunity to a status type after it ends; statuses never apply in the final 10s of a duel; every status has a visible timer ring.
    80→
    81→ABILITIES: 3 per Order, each with a cooldown ring and a label. The first use of each ability in a duel starts at 50% cooldown.
    82→
    83→ORDERS (4 portrait cards, all free via play):
    84→ SCHOLAR (Order of the Quill). Passive Marginalia: your first mistake each duel costs no Seal.
    85→   Eye / Augur: reveal the correct digit in the selected empty cell. CD 40s.
    86→   Key / Unseal: clear all statuses on your board and become immune for 6s. CD 30s.
    87→   Quill / Fair Copy: auto-fill correct pencil candidates for the selected box. CD 20s.
    88→ EXECUTIONER (Order of the Axe). Passive Last Rites: your claims deal +1 when the opponent has 3 or fewer Seals.
    89→   Dagger / Sever: Chain the empty cell in the opponent's most nearly complete unit for 8s. CD 25s.
    90→   Hourglass / Hush: opponent's number pad is dead for 2.5s. CD 35s.
    91→   Axe / Reckoning: your next claim within 20s deals +1. CD 45s.
    92→ APOTHECARY (Order of the Vial). Passive Distiller: statuses you apply last 2s longer.
    93→   Vial / Smudge: blur 5 of the opponent's placed digits for 7s. CD 24s.
    94→   Cup / Tincture: restore 1 Seal (max 7). CD 60s, 2 uses per duel.
    95→   Censer / Miasma: apply Miasma for 10s. CD 28s.
    96→ WARDEN (Order of the Lantern). Passive Bulwark: the first incoming status each duel is negated.
    97→   Shield / Ward: negate the next incoming status within 15s. CD 40s.
    98→   Lantern / Mirror: reflect the next incoming status back within 10s. CD 45s.
    99→   Bar / Quarantine: apply Quarantine to a unit you pick on the opponent's board. CD 35s.
   100→ Unlocks: Scholar and Executioner at start. Apothecary after Folio II. Warden after Folio IV (or 40 Ink-Seals of Ledger score, whichever you design, but document it).
